OUR STORY

Baked with love,

Miksweets is a small sweets business based in Quezon City, created for people who believe that good desserts bring people together.

From a simple afternoon snack to a thoughtful gift, our goal is to make every order feel special. We focus on cozy flavors, inviting presentation, and the happiness that comes with sharing something sweet.
